# Live screen references

Captured from the public production app at https://www.miez.news on 9 October 2026. These are unmodified browser screenshots with the app's light or dark preference selected. Desktop captures are scrolled to show rows and reading sections rather than letting the lead photo fill the viewport. Mobile captures show the top of the feed.

The app is a visual example, not a dependency or a required product template. Its name, content, navigation and domain colours do not belong to the shared taste.

`capture.json` records the source URLs, viewport sizes, themes and capture time. Screenshots include third-party headlines and publisher photographs as displayed by the live app. They are visual references, not a collection of reusable news images.
