7 October 2026

The cloth is worn through at the corners. Underneath, the board is still firm. I cut four small patches instead of replacing the cover.

A loose spine

4 October 2026

The sewing held. The glue didn't. A little warmth, a patient brush, and the sections came apart without losing a page.
