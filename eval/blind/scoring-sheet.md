# Human scoring sheet

Keep key/ closed until scoring is complete. Review the same task, viewport, theme and text size across all samples. Check normal and enlarged pages before rating. Inspect full-size images and use the live HTML if interaction affects your judgment.

Scores are independent, 0-4. Zero prevents the task or misses the brief; one needs major correction; two works with visible friction; three is clear with small corrections; four works across checked states and fits this subject. Do not combine the five categories into one weighted score.

- Usability: can the operator find a due sample and predict the next action?
- Product fit: does this look like a municipal sampling service and a useful laboratory queue?
- Feels like Flavius: do subject, hierarchy and expression feel authored in his taste?
- Mechanical/accessibility soundness: names, contrast, focus, reflow, readable line spacing and truthful states.
- Correction effort: record estimated minutes and concrete edits separately from preference. Zero effort means ready to ship; do not treat a guess as measured time.

A functional or accessibility failure is a failed gate. Keep its ratings for diagnosis, but do not call that candidate ready to ship or average the failure into a preference total. Automated passes have a bounded scope. Human visual failures and unverified checks remain separate.
