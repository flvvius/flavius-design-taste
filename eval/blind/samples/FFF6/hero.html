Our methods

Start with the monitoring question.

A sampling plan identifies where to collect water and what to analyze. Tell us about the sites you manage, the purpose of monitoring and any existing requirements so we can discuss the scope.

Collection

The plan sets out the sampling locations and collection approach. Sample records connect each collection to its site.

Laboratory analysis

Samples proceed through laboratory processing and review. The analysis requested in the plan determines the methods used.
