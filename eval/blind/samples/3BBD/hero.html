Methods

A sampling plan connects the council's monitoring needs with field collection and laboratory analysis.

01 / Field collection

Agree what to sample, and where.

Discuss the monitoring purpose and sampling sites with Fieldwater. The plan sets out the collection work and the information to record with each sample.

02 / Laboratory analysis

Match the analysis to the question.

Samples go to the laboratory for analysis. Discuss the proposed methods when arranging the plan, so the council knows what the results will cover.
