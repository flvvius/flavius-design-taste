# Shared signature

The portfolio and Pricele share an attitude rather than a font stack. Both let the subject determine the page's character. One is a handwritten listening bar; the other is a price game composed like a broadsheet. The hypothesis that this is an authored, useful interface practice is supported by those decisions, but its effect on visitors remains an inference.

Observed in both projects:

- Warm paper and ink roles, with one expressive accent. Domain colors can communicate additional meaning.
- Type has a deliberate job. Schoolbell writes the portfolio; Pricele assigns display, UI and figures to separate faces.
- A real grid establishes alignment. Objects can overhang it; rail headings can occupy it. Small screens adapt the composition.
- Most reading content belongs on the page. Material notes, game figures, controls and overlays can earn their own boundaries.
- Marks and imagery refer to the subject. They do not come from a generic inventory of feature icons.
- Optional motion has reduced-motion handling. A control response and an occasional drawing do not need the same duration.

[Portfolio evidence](../references/own-work/portofolio.md) and [Pricele evidence](../references/own-work/pricele.md) identify pinned source files and capture provenance.

Direction-specific choices stay separate. Editorial calm uses sans hierarchy, semibold titles, quiet rows, graphite actions and short state feedback. Personal room uses handwriting, material objects and paper or navy settings. Pricele's serif headlines, standing heads and broadsheet rails are recipes. They do not establish a third preset or a mandatory type pairing.

The shared principles are a practical interpretation of the source and existing directions. Each [principle](principles.md) labels its origin. Claims about what feels authored, what deserves emphasis or how much decoration helps are inferences, even when the implementation example is directly observed.

Apply the signature by choosing a hierarchy, density and visual idea that explains this subject. Do not import a shelf into unrelated software or force a price game into a settings template. Keep the client's useful identity, content and behavior. Cream backgrounds, hairlines, serif display type, Inter and handwritten marks are all permitted when they have a reason.

Success needs both usable behavior and a recognizable authorial choice. Automated checks can measure some mechanical failures. Flavius supplies the final judgment on taste.
